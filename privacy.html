Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy explains what information Riftnewt Games ("we", "us") collects and how we use it. The short version: this website collects nothing about you, and the tools we use to publish our videos only touch our own accounts.

This website

  • We do not have user accounts, sign-up forms, comments or a shop.
  • We do not use cookies, advertising trackers or analytics.
  • Our web host may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested) for security and to keep the site running. We do not use these logs to identify visitors.
  • If you email us, we receive your email address and your message. We use them only to reply, and we do not sell or share them.

Publishing our videos

We make short videos about our games and publish them on our own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts. To schedule and upload them, we use a publishing tool that runs on our own computer and connects to those accounts through each platform's official sign-in (OAuth).

  • We connect only accounts that Riftnewt Games owns. We do not connect, access or post to anyone else's account.
  • The tool receives an access token for each connected account. Tokens are stored on our own computer, are used only to upload and schedule our own videos and read basic information about our own posts, and are never shared or sold.
  • We do not collect personal information about our viewers, subscribers, followers or people who comment on our videos. What each platform collects about you when you watch is covered by that platform's own privacy policy.
  • We can disconnect any platform at any time, and you can review or revoke our access to an account you own from that platform's security settings.

YouTube API Services

Our publishing tool uses YouTube API Services to upload our own videos. By using our YouTube content you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access that our tool holds to a Google account can be revoked at any time in the Google security settings.

Children

Our games and videos are for a general audience. This website does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. If a child has emailed us, tell us and we will delete it.

Links to other sites

We link to other sites, such as Roblox, Steam, YouTube, Instagram and TikTok. They have their own privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the date above.
